Microsoft MVP | The big brother AI builders never had | AI Engineering & Leadership | Founder of MasterDexter and Buildership

Close to a decade in AI. 10,000+ engineers trained. Most of them never shipped anything.

I was teaching in the wrong order.

That question cost me years. In 2025 I finally had the answer. I flipped the sequence. Pick a real problem first. Learn only what that build demands. Ship it live. Measure traction with real users. That became PILOT.

In 2026, the first cohort built entirely on PILOT shipped 6 production-grade AI systems in 8 weeks. All deployed. All live.

## Background

Close to a decade in AI. Started freelancing in 2017. Built HemansAI, an AI product studio, in 2018.

In 2019 launched the Machine Learning Engineer Bootcamp. 20 students. Live sessions every week. I rebuilt the curriculum every time the market moved.

Co-founded Dextar in 2020. AI engineering and consulting across energy, healthcare, life sciences, and defence. This is where the real production system patterns came from.

MTech in Data Science completed in 2021. Research thesis: knowledge tracing across 130 million student interactions. The data showed that learning sequence predicts outcomes more than content volume. That finding is inside every PILOT cohort.

Microsoft MVP awarded in 2022. God Level Data Science repo crossed 1,000 stars the same year. Delivered an 8-hour AI training session for law enforcement officers in India.

Keynoted Google DevFest Indore in 2023 (100 attendees) and again in 2025 (1,000 attendees). Talk title: "How to Learn AI Fast?" The answer was not to learn faster. It was to learn in the right order.

In 2026, ran the first cohort fully built on the PILOT framework. Engineers assigned GitHub issues like a real engineering team. Pull requests reviewed. 6 artifacts. 8 weeks. All deployed. All live.
